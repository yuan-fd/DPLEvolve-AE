# Level 1 Evidence Index

This index is intentionally empty until reviewed calibration evidence is
promoted.

When Level 1 calibration records are added, list only stable evidence ids,
source-start ids, review outcomes, and portable artifact references. Generated
metrics/logs may come from a mechanism-sweep round under
`$DPL_EVOLVE_STATE_ROOT/<round_id>/teacher_rounds/` or from a diagnostic archive
under `$DPL_EVOLVE_STATE_ROOT/calibrations/<calibration_id>/`; tracked summaries
should cite those paths through the environment-variable prefix.
